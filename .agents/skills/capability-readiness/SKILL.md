# Capability Readiness

Apply `../_shared/career-contract.md`. This Skill owns
`60-capability-readiness` and `readiness.gap`, `readiness.note`,
`readiness.session`, and `readiness.assessment`. Before a canonical write or gate
judgment, load `system/seeds/authorities/60-capability-readiness.md`. Knowledge
and practice gaps close only through a verified passing Retest; production-
evidence gaps close only through medium or strong grounded Work evidence.

## Modes

- **diagnose** defines one specific gap against a target.
- **learn** uses active recall and practice to close one gap.
- **study-paper** records a source-grounded note tied to a gap.
- **practice** runs a coached or strict interview session.
- **assess** evaluates readiness against explicit criteria and evidence.
- **retest** checks prior blockers with fresh work.

## Fast path

Work one target and the smallest blocking gap at a time. Coached learning and
ordinary practice need no reviewer: use a short probe, diagnose the miss, drill
it, and schedule a fresh retest. Do not generate an exhaustive question bank or
mark a gap closed from reading, a completed lab, a green tool, or a polished
answer.

## Interview preparation model

- **Public Surface** references the current Career Communication claim/profile
  and optional JD; Readiness does not become a second wording authority.
- **Minimum Credible Narrative** is a 60–90 second opening covering problem,
  personal responsibility, key tradeoff, result, and boundary, with at most two
  or three deliberate **Disclosure Hooks**.
- Rank hooks by role relevance, contribution differentiation, and support.
  Inference and unknowns stay explicit; incomplete artifacts change confidence,
  not whether user-confirmed real work may be practiced.
- **Universal First Questions** come from the public surface or ordinary domain
  expectations. **Triggered Follow-up Branches** may introduce project details
  only after a visible hook or prior candidate answer.
- Keep formulas, defaults, source locations, and low-probability details in a
  **Detail Appendix** for on-demand recall.

## Workflow

1. Name the target baseline or JD delta, pass criteria, and required evidence.
2. Keep one reusable project draft at `notes/projects/<project-key>.md` when
   purpose and lifecycle match. Keep papers, cross-project topics, dated
   Sessions, Assessments, and Gaps separate; never clone a draft per JD.
3. Build the draft from Public Surface, Minimum Credible Narrative, Universal
   First Questions, up to three Disclosure Hooks, Triggered Follow-ups, and the
   Detail Appendix. Keep knowledge, practice, and production evidence distinct.
4. For strict practice or `assess`, send only the Internal Evidence Packet to
   `evidence-auditor` and only the Public Interview Packet to
   `blind-interviewer`; validate both results before adjudication. Every probe
   cites its exact public-surface, JD, or prior-answer basis. Never expose
   evidence posture, source archaeology, private notes, expected answers, or
   hidden rubrics. Ordinary learning and coached practice should not pay this
   reviewer cost.
5. Preserve original questions, answers, criteria, reviewer results, and
   blockers. Do not repair an answer after the fact. Invalid, unavailable,
   stale, or leaked review falls back without producing `ready`.
6. Derive readiness only from the configured baseline, target delta, required
   sessions, valid reviewers when required, retests, and evidence. Finish with
   the top blocker and next retest condition.

Write only Capability Readiness records. Send new attributable work facts to
Career Evidence and application decisions to Opportunity Decision.
